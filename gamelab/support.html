2. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Multigames free to play?

Multigames offers free gameplay. Certain optional rewards may be available by watching advertisements.

Why am I seeing advertisements?

Advertisements help support the development and maintenance of the application. Some advertisements provide optional in-game rewards.

What if I don't receive my reward?

Make sure the rewarded advertisement finishes successfully. If the reward is not granted, try restarting the application.

If the issue continues, contact support and include the game name, device model, and a description of the problem.

What should I do if a game freezes?

Try closing and reopening the application. Make sure you are using the latest available version.

If the issue persists, please contact support with the steps needed to reproduce the problem.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Some gameplay features may work offline. Features that depend on advertisements or online services may require an internet connection.

How can I suggest a new game?

We welcome suggestions and feedback. Send us an email with your ideas for future mini-games or improvements.

3. Reporting a Bug

If you encounter an error or unexpected behavior, please provide:

  • The name of the mini-game.
  • Your device model.
  • Your operating system version.
  • A description of the issue.
  • The steps needed to reproduce it.
  • A screenshot, if available.

This information helps us investigate and resolve problems more effectively.
