Arena Battle Game

Build your gladiator, master your equipment and enter the arena. Fight asynchronous battles, improve your warrior and climb your way from an unknown fighter to a legend of the arena.

Discover Rudis Game Features

Fight for Glory

In Rudis, every fighter begins with little more than ambition. Train your gladiator, improve your attributes, collect new weapons and armor, and prepare for battle against fighters from across the arena. Every victory brings new rewards, experience and another step toward glory.

Your Path Through the Arena

Build, equip, fight and grow stronger after every battle.

1

Build Your Fighter

Create your gladiator and develop the attributes that define your fighting style, including strength, dexterity, health and defense.

2

Equip for Battle

Collect swords, daggers, axes, spears, shields, helmets and armor. Choose the right equipment before entering the arena.

3

Enter the Arena

Face other fighters in asynchronous battles. Your build, equipment and combat attributes determine how your warrior performs.

4

Claim Your Rewards

Earn experience, coins and equipment. Improve your gladiator and prepare for stronger opponents.

Asynchronous Arena Battles

No Waiting for Opponents

Battles are asynchronous, so you can enter the arena without waiting for another player to be online at the same moment.

Watch Every Fight

Experience your battles through animated arena replays and see how your fighter's equipment and attributes perform in combat.

Different Fighting Styles

Weapons behave differently in battle. Range, attack speed, damage and defensive equipment all influence the fight.

Weapons & Equipment

Every piece of gear can change the way your gladiator performs.

Swords & Daggers

Balanced weapons for gladiators who value reliable attacks, mobility and speed.

Axes & Heavy Weapons

Powerful equipment designed to deliver devastating hits at the cost of slower attacks.

Spears

Fight from greater range and force opponents to close the distance before they can strike.

Shields & Armor

Strengthen your defenses with shields, helmets and armor and survive longer against powerful opponents.

Become Stronger

Victory is only the beginning. Gain experience, level up and decide how your gladiator grows. Improve your combat attributes, unlock better equipment and create a fighter capable of surviving increasingly dangerous battles.
