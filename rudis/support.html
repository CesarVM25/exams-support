Need help in the arena? Find answers to common questions or contact us directly for support with Rudis: Battle Arena.

How Can We Help?

Find information about common issues below. If you still need assistance, contact VillaLabs directly.

Account & Sign-In

If you are having trouble signing in, make sure you are using the same sign-in method associated with your Rudis account. If the problem continues, contact us and describe the issue.

Battles & Progress

If a battle, reward, achievement or progression update does not appear correctly, restart the game and check your internet connection. Contact us if the issue persists.

Rewards & Equipment

If you believe a reward, item or equipment upgrade was not correctly added to your account, contact support with as much information about the affected battle or reward as possible.

Advertisements

Rudis may offer optional rewarded advertisements. If an advertisement does not load or a reward is not granted after completion, please contact us with details about the issue.

Technical Issues

If Rudis crashes, freezes or behaves unexpectedly, try closing and reopening the app. When contacting us, include your device model and operating system version whenever possible.

Delete Your Account

If you want to permanently delete your Rudis account and associated account data, visit our account deletion page.

Contact Support

Can't find the answer you need?

Contact VillaLabs and tell us what happened. Please include your Rudis account email when relevant and any details that may help us investigate the issue.
