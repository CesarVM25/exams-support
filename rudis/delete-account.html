Delete Your Rudis Account

If you would like to permanently delete your Rudis: Arena Battle Game account, you can request account deletion by contacting VillaLabs.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to villalabs.app@gmail.com using, whenever possible, the same email address associated with your Rudis account.

Please use the subject: Rudis Account Deletion Request

In your message, include enough information for us to identify your account, such as:

  • The email address associated with your Rudis account.
  • Your character or username, if applicable.
  • A clear statement that you want your Rudis account deleted.

What will be deleted

Once your request has been verified and processed, your Rudis account and the personal data associated with it will be deleted or anonymized, where applicable.

This may include:

  • Account information.
  • Character data.
  • Game progress and level.
  • Experience and statistics.
  • Inventory and equipment.
  • Virtual currencies and rewards.
  • Battle-related data linked to your account.
  • Other data associated with your Rudis profile.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained when required by law or when necessary for legitimate purposes such as fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or the protection of our rights.

Any data retained for these purposes will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Important information

Account deletion is permanent. Once the deletion process has been completed, your character, progress, inventory, virtual currencies, rewards, and other associated game data may not be recoverable.

Never send us your password, full payment card details, or other sensitive financial information.
