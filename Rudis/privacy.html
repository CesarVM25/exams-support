Privacy Policy for Rudis

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used, stored, and protected in connection with Rudis: Arena Battle Game (hereinafter "Rudis", the "Application", "App", or the "Service").

The privacy of our users is important to us. We process personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, or GDPR) and applicable Spanish data protection legislation.

1. Data Controller

The controller responsible for processing personal data in connection with Rudis is the developer operating under the brand:

VillaLabs

Contact email:
villalabs.app@gmail.com

Website:
villalabs.app

Users may use this email address to submit privacy-related inquiries or exercise their rights regarding personal data.

2. Data We May Collect

Depending on how Rudis is used, we may collect and process different categories of information.

2.1. Account Data

When a user creates an account, we may process information such as:

  • Email address.
  • Internal user identifier.
  • Name or username, where applicable.
  • Login method.
  • Information necessary to verify or maintain the account.

When an external authentication provider such as Google or Apple is used, we may receive an identifier and certain basic information authorized by the user.

We do not receive the password used by the user to access services such as Google or Apple directly.

3. Game Data

To provide the game's functionality and maintain persistent game progress, we may store information related to the user's activity within Rudis.

This may include:

  • Created characters.
  • Character names.
  • Character race or type.
  • Level.
  • Experience.
  • Statistics.
  • Equipment.
  • Weapons.
  • Armor.
  • Shields.
  • Helmets.
  • Items obtained.
  • Virtual currency.
  • Rewards.
  • Progress.
  • History of certain actions.
  • Battle information.
  • Battle results.
  • Information required to replay battles.
  • Settings associated with the account.

This data is necessary to maintain the user's progress and provide the various features of Rudis.

4. Battle Data

Rudis includes battle systems and asynchronous player-versus-player (PvP) functionality.

To provide these features, we may store data such as:

  • Battle identifier.
  • Participants.
  • Equipment used.
  • Relevant statistics.
  • Events occurring during the battle.
  • Damage dealt.
  • Battle result.
  • Winner.
  • Rewards.
  • Information necessary to generate or replay the battle.

Some of this information may be displayed to another player when necessary to provide PvP functionality.

For example, other players may be able to view certain public information about the character they are fighting against.

5. Technical Data

When a user accesses or uses Rudis, we may automatically receive certain technical information.

Depending on the device and services used, this information may include:

  • Device type.
  • Operating system.
  • Operating system version.
  • Application version.
  • Language.
  • Approximate country or region.
  • IP address.
  • Connection information.
  • Technical logs.
  • Application errors.
  • Performance information.
  • Technical identifiers.

This data may be used to ensure the proper operation of the Service, diagnose errors, improve stability, and protect Rudis against abuse.

6. Advertising

Rudis may display advertising provided by third-party services.

Rudis may currently use services provided by Appodeal and advertising networks integrated through the Appodeal platform.

These providers may process device-related information in order to display, measure, and manage advertisements.

Depending on the device, user settings, consent provided, and applicable law, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • IP address.
  • Device information.
  • Operating system information.
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Interactions with advertisements.
  • Ad impressions.
  • Clicks.
  • Information necessary to prevent advertising fraud.

The availability and personalization of advertisements will depend on the user's consent choices and applicable law.

7. Rewarded Advertisements

Rudis may allow users to voluntarily view certain advertisements in exchange for in-game rewards.

For example, a user may choose to view an advertisement in order to:

  • Reduce waiting times.
  • Improve a reward.
  • Receive certain in-game benefits.

When this functionality is used, we may process the information necessary to verify that the advertisement was successfully viewed and to grant the corresponding reward.

8. Advertising Tracking on iOS

On Apple devices, certain forms of tracking across applications and websites may require authorization through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework.

Where required, Rudis will request this authorization using the mechanism provided by iOS.

The user may accept or reject this authorization.

Rejecting tracking will not prevent the user from accessing the essential functionality of Rudis.

Users may subsequently change their preferences through the privacy settings of their device.

9. Advertising Consent

Where required by applicable law, we will request the user's consent before using certain identifiers or technologies for personalized advertising.

Users may reject personalized advertising where this option is available.

This does not necessarily mean that advertisements will no longer be displayed. Advertisements may still appear but may be less personalized.

The available options may depend on the user's country, device, and advertising provider.

  • Maintaining game progress.

  • Managing inventory and equipment.
  • Processing battles.
  • Providing PvP functionality.
  • Generating and replaying battles.
  • Granting rewards.
  • Maintaining virtual currencies and resources.
  • Providing advertising.
  • Managing rewarded advertisements.
  • Detecting errors.
  • Improving stability and performance.
  • Preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Protecting account security.
  • Providing user support.
  • Complying with legal obligations.

Data will not be used for purposes incompatible with those for which it was collected unless there is a lawful basis permitting such use.
